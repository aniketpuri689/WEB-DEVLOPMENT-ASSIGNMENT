Topic 08: Audio, Video & iFrame

Notes from Class:

  • <audio controls>: Plays sound. The controls word is needed to show the play/pause button.
  • <video controls width="300">: Plays video files like .mp4.
  • <source src="..." type="..."> specifies the media link.
  • <iframe src="...">: Shows another webpage inside our page.
